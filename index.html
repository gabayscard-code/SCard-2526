WHAT IS THE SCHOLARS' CARD?

The Scholar’s Card (SCard) is an initiative designed to extend meaningful benefits, such as discounts and exclusive offers, to scholars and Gabayanos within the Atenean community. More than just a card, SCard represents our collective commitment to supporting academic journeys and uplifting one another through shared opportunities.
